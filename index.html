Quarterly estimated taxes. As a self-employed plumber, the IRS expects payments four times a year — not just on April 15. Missing these means underpayment penalties. The amounts below are estimates based on your YTD net income and the tax rate in Settings.

Pay federal estimates at IRS Direct Pay (free). Ohio state estimates at tax.ohio.gov. This app gives federal estimates only — Ohio adds roughly 3-4% on top.
